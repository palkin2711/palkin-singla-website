Social performance systems

Content connected to audience stage—not posted in isolation.

New strategy documents show how organic content, prospecting creative, warm-audience retargeting and lead capture can work as one coordinated social funnel.

01 · ProspectingMeta funnel

Creative testing across cold audience layers.

Interest and demographic audiences, purchase lookalikes, CRM-based high-value audiences and engagement lookalikes were mapped into separate ad sets with clear exclusions.

  • Three creative variations per ad set
  • Purchase-event optimization
  • Seven-day learning and review cycle
Open Meta campaign structure PDF ↗
02 · RetargetingWarm audiences

Different messages for different intent signals.

Website depth, video views, checkout initiation and add-to-cart windows were separated so creative could match each user’s relationship with the brand.

  • 30, 60 and 180-day audience windows
  • Carousel and catalogue formats
  • Overlap exclusions between ad sets
03 · Cross-channel planSalescalator

A three-tier journey from discovery to qualified action.

Search, LinkedIn and YouTube supported discovery; Facebook lead ads and LinkedIn messaging captured higher intent; email nurtured existing leads toward conversion.

5Paid formats
