Performance deep dives

Patent Prints, C2 Paint and Her Smart Choice—now with actual analysis.

The percentage comparisons below are calculated from figures visible in the supplied Ads Manager screenshots. They are relative efficiency comparisons, not invented before/after claims.

Patent Prints · Gift & décor eCommerce · ~6 months

Scale came from DPA and media-mix structures; static creative showed stronger cost efficiency.

509Top DPA purchases
$51.65Top DPA cost / purchase
$37.07Static LEGO cost / purchase
387Media Mix purchases
  • The visible DPA - All Products ad produced 509 purchases at $51.65 cost per purchase.
  • A Static - LEGO Man + LEGO Brick ad recorded 112 purchases at $37.07, about 28.2% lower cost per purchase than the top DPA benchmark.
  • The Statics (12/8) ad set recorded 137 purchases at $40.46, about 21.7% lower than $51.65.
  • The account therefore shows a useful balance: DPA carried higher purchase volume, while selected static concepts delivered lower visible purchase cost.
  • This is a cross-creative comparison from the supplied lifetime view; revenue and ROAS are not claimed because they are not shown in the evidence.
C2 Paint · US paint brand · ~1 year

Retargeting supplied volume, while a C2 Guard purchase ad set showed the strongest visible cost efficiency.

441Website visitors purchases
$40.94Website visitors CPP
$20.85C2 Guard CPP
167Retargeting purchases
  • A01 Website Visitors (90D) recorded 441 purchases at $40.94 cost per purchase.
  • A06 Retargeting (Stacked) recorded 167 purchases at $42.95.
  • C2 Guard | Purchase | US | June recorded 47 purchases at $20.85—about 49.1% lower cost per purchase than the 90-day website-visitor ad set.
  • Compared with the visible broad new-creative test at $65.91 cost per purchase, the $20.85 C2 Guard set is about 68.4% lower on this metric.
  • Full Spectrum Creative Test recorded 19 purchases at $38.69, showing a second creative-testing path close to the mature retargeting benchmark.
  • The practical takeaway is to preserve high-volume retargeting while testing controlled budget increases on lower-CPP product/creative combinations.
Her Smart Choice · US healthcare · ~1 year

Call campaigns and schedule campaigns show clear cost differences by offer and funnel stage.

5,087Traffic clicks
$0.50Traffic CPC
129Abortion-pill calls
$14.14Cost / call
  • The main traffic campaign recorded 5,087 link clicks at $0.50; a Women's Health traffic campaign recorded 1,091 clicks at $0.44.
  • Abortion Pill / Telemedicine recorded 129 calls at $14.14 per call.
  • A separate LA call campaign recorded 152 calls at $20.90; the $14.14 campaign is about 32.3% lower cost per call.
  • The booking-calls campaign recorded 159 calls at $21.31; $14.14 is about 33.6% lower.
  • Website Visitors | Leads recorded 34 schedules at $34.21, about 40.7% lower cost per schedule than the visible STD testing set at $57.68.
  • The evidence supports separating traffic, calls and schedule conversions in reporting rather than blending them into one CPL.
