Privacy & website data.

This page explains the information this website may receive when you browse, contact me or follow an external link.

Contact enquiries

If you submit the website contact form or contact me by email or phone, the information you provide is used to review and respond to your enquiry and to communicate about the requested work.

Analytics

The website contains an optional Google Analytics 4 integration. When analytics is enabled, it may collect standard usage information such as pages viewed, referral source, device/browser information and interaction events. Analytics is used to understand website performance rather than to sell personal information.

External services and links

Some pages link to third-party services such as LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, portfolio/client websites and downloadable documents. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Data requests

If you want to ask about information you submitted through this website, use the contact page. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the general enquiry form.
