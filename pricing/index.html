Pricing FAQ

Simple pricing with scope confirmed before work starts.

Are these fixed prices?

They are starting package prices. If the account, website or content scope is significantly larger, I will confirm a custom price before work begins.

Does the price include advertising spend?

No. Google, Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok media spend is paid directly to the advertising platform and is separate from management fees.

How does the festive 20% discount work?

During configured festive offer windows, this page automatically shows a 20% reduced package price. The offer applies to listed service fees booked during the active period.

Can I create a custom bundle?

Yes. If the listed combinations do not fit, choose the closest package and send your requirements. A custom scope can be built around the channels you actually need.

Not sure what to choose?

Start with the business goal and I’ll recommend the smallest useful scope.

You do not need to buy every channel. The best package is the one that fixes the most important constraint first.
