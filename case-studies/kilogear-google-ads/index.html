KILOGEAR · Google Ads · Weighted fitness apparel

Building branded demand and non-brand discovery for weighted workout clothing.

This Google Ads case study covers a three-campaign structure for KILOGEAR: Brand Search, Non-Brand Search and Performance Max. The account targeted people looking for weighted clothing, weighted shirts, wearable resistance apparel and related fitness products while protecting branded demand through a dedicated Search campaign.

Keyword insight

Branded searches showed clear demand, but non-brand expansion created new category discovery.

  • The two main brand keywords generated 94 clicks with CTRs of 30.57% and 19.41%.
  • Non-brand demand clustered around weighted clothing, weighted shirts, workout weight vests and training apparel.
  • Several emerging keywords such as weighted gym clothes and weighted workout clothes showed double-digit CTRs at smaller volume.
  • The account structure therefore separated people already looking for KILOGEAR from shoppers still exploring the weighted-apparel category.

Search-term intelligence

The query mix reveals both branded interest and competitive/product comparison behavior.

The search-term export included 100 query rows. The most visible terms included KILOGEAR variants, weighted-shirt searches and competitor/product-comparison queries.

High-volume query themes

  • kilogear — 36 clicks from 98 impressions.
  • kilos gear — 32 clicks from 115 impressions.
  • weighted shirt — 8 clicks from 82 impressions.
  • weighted clothing — 6 clicks from 53 impressions.
  • weighted shirt for men — 4 clicks from 23 impressions.

Comparison and competitor signals

  • Queries included the carry weighted vest, omorpho weighted vest, gravcore weighted vest and titin weighted shirt.
  • This shows shoppers were comparing weighted-apparel alternatives, creating useful conquest and differentiation opportunities.
  • The supplied search-term rows show 0 reported conversions, so these queries are used here as demand/intent evidence rather than sales evidence.
Device, timing & demographic behavior

Mobile dominated traffic, with strongest visible audience concentration among men 35–44.

Mobile-first performance

  • 92.3% of clicks came from mobile phones.
  • Mobile also represented 84.9% of impressions and 81.4% of cost.
  • Computers represented 15.6% of cost but only 4.0% of clicks.
  • For this account, mobile landing-page speed and checkout experience are especially important.

Audience timing & demographics

  • The visible heatmap shows activity pockets around Sunday midday, Monday early morning and several evening periods during the week.
  • The darkest known demographic segment appears among men aged 35–44, with additional reach across 25–54 age groups.
  • The dashboard notes that age/gender data covers 56% of impressions, so demographic conclusions should remain directional.
Ad relevance

The most-shown Search ad matched the non-brand product category directly.

The ad headline focused on Weighted Workout Clothing, Wearable Resistance Clothing and Weighted Fitness Apparel, with description copy explaining micro-weighted workout clothing and resistance for movement and performance.

Most-shown Search ad

  • 1,673 impressions
  • 120 clicks
  • 7.17% CTR
  • Direct alignment with the Non Brand Search campaign and the weighted-clothing keyword set.

Why the copy works

  • It explains the product category immediately instead of assuming users already know the brand.
  • It connects the weighted-apparel feature to an understandable benefit: adding resistance to movement.
  • Sitelink options for Men, Women and Girls give category-level navigation directly from the ad.
