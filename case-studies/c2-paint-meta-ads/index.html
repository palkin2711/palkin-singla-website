Product-specific purchase campaign

C2 Guard: 71 purchases at $31.71 cost per purchase.

C2 Guard and Full Spectrum spent almost the same amount—$2,251.67 versus $2,254.89—but C2 Guard generated 71 purchases versus 34.

  • C2 Guard’s cost per purchase was about 52.2% lower.
  • It generated about 2.09× as many purchases at essentially the same spend.
  • Versus the Room Visual campaign at $52.32 per purchase, C2 Guard was about 39.4% lower on purchase cost.

Retargeting scale

Retargeting remained the account’s purchase-volume engine.

  • BT - Retargeting: 631 purchases at $43.39 cost per purchase.
  • The campaign delivered 1.73M impressions against 328K reach, producing a 5.27 frequency.
  • That frequency makes creative refresh and audience saturation important operational considerations.
Audience depth

The 90-day website audience was efficient, but heavily saturated.

  • A01 - Website Visitors (90D): 443 purchases at $40.90, 4.05× Meta-reported purchase ROAS.
  • Frequency reached 11.72, indicating repeated exposure to a comparatively small audience.
  • A06 - Retargeting (Stacked) - Catalog: 178 purchases at $43.08 with only 2.42 frequency, trading slightly higher purchase cost for a much broader delivery pattern.

C2 Guard vs Full Spectrum

At the ad-set level, C2 Guard’s $23.58 purchase cost was about 51.3% lower than Full Spectrum’s $48.44. Its Meta-reported ROAS was also higher at 3.61× versus 2.94×.

C2 Guard vs broad C2 Paint

The $23.58 C2 Guard ad set was about 76.2% lower in purchase cost than the $99.20 broad C2 Paint new-creative ad set. The two were not controlled like-for-like tests, but the gap is large enough to guide the next testing priority.

Testing framework visible in the account

Retargeting, catalog, product campaigns and new-creative prospecting were separated instead of blended together.

1. Retargeting for scale

Website visitors and catalog retargeting carried the largest purchase volume. This gave the account a stable benchmark for purchase cost and creative fatigue.

2. Product-specific C2 Guard testing

C2 Guard campaigns and ads consistently produced lower purchase-cost signals than several general C2 Paint / room-visual tests in the supplied data.

3. Creative-format diversity

Expert video, UGC, catalog, single-image and room-visual creative were all tested. That makes the account less dependent on one creative format.

4. Broad new-creative prospecting

New 2026 broad ad sets give a clean place to test fresh hooks. The data currently favors C2 Guard over broader C2 Paint creative on purchase cost.
