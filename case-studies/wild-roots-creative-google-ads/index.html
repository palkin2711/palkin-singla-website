Devices

Mobile generated most clicks, while computers represented a larger share of spend than their click share.

Click distribution

  • Mobile phones: 83.9% of clicks.
  • Tablets: 5.9%.
  • Computers: 10.2%.
  • TV screens: 0.0% in the screenshot.

Cost & impressions

  • Cost: 76.2% mobile, 2.9% tablet, 20.7% computer, 0.2% TV.
  • Impressions: 82.9% mobile, 7.1% tablet, 9.7% computer, 0.4% TV.
  • The supplied screenshot therefore shows different cost and click distributions by device.

Audience & timing

The account reaches multiple age groups, with visible impression intensity among women 35–44 and 65+.

Demographics

  • The heatmap is based on 66% of impressions with known gender and age.
  • The strongest visible female cells are 35–44 and 65+.
  • Male 35–44 also shows a comparatively strong cell.
  • These are impression patterns and do not establish purchase propensity.

Day & hour

  • Delivery is visible across the week.
  • The heatmap is relatively sparse overnight and shows more activity during daytime and evening hours.
  • A particularly dark cell appears around late morning on Friday.
  • The chart is impression-based, not conversion-based.
