How to read the work

Different Google Ads objectives use different success metrics.

Lead-generation accounts may use calls or forms; app campaigns use installs; YouTube campaigns use views and view rate; eCommerce and Search accounts may emphasize clicks, conversions or purchase signals. Each case study therefore keeps unlike outcomes separate instead of combining them into a misleading total.

Reporting note: metrics come from the source exports and Google Ads screenshots supplied for each project. Derived figures are labelled as derived or estimated. Where a source did not support revenue, lead quality or conversions, the case study does not invent those outcomes.
