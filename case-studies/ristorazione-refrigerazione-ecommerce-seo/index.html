Italian eCommerce SEO

Growing organic discovery for professional catering and refrigeration equipment.

Ristorazione Refrigerazione is an eCommerce site for professional catering and kitchen equipment serving restaurants, cafés, bars, bakeries, pizzerias and other commercial food-service businesses. The supplied report documents search visibility, backlinks, referring domains and commercial product-query performance.

Work represented

  • eCommerce category and product-intent SEO.
  • Commercial keyword targeting for professional equipment.
  • Search Console monitoring and query analysis.
  • Backlink/referring-domain growth and organic visibility.

Top-query examples shown

  • 3.3 avg. position — friggitrici ad aria professionale per ristoranti
  • 3.3 — kit pizzeria forno elettrico
  • 5.4 — fornelli elettrici professionali
  • 6.2 — fornetto scalda pizza
  • 7.5 — sterilizzatrice a freddo / ifi arredamenti
