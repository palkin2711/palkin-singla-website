Lucas E Kadi · Google Ads · YouTube Video Views

Scaling video reach for Lucas E Kadi with high-volume YouTube view campaigns.

This case study analyzes the supplied Google Ads video campaign exports and account screenshots for Lucas E Kadi music-video promotion. The focus is on TrueView views, view rate, CPV, device mix, audience delivery, day-and-hour patterns and in-stream retention.

Top video assets

One Lucas E Kadi compilation campaign generated the largest view count, while another showed the highest view rate.

Highest view volume

  • “SE NAMORAR FOSSE BOM / E ESSA BOCA AI” delivered 146,989 TrueView views.
  • Reported view rate: 43.84%.
  • Spend: R$827.83.

Highest view rate

  • “TALISMÃ / FOI DEUS / ADORO AMAR VOCÊ” recorded the highest supplied view rate at 76.70%.
  • It generated 98,617 views on R$777.23 spend.
  • High view rate is a delivery metric; it does not by itself establish downstream audience quality or business impact.

Devices

Mobile generated the majority of clicks, while TV screens represented a large share of impressions and spend.

Click distribution

  • Mobile phones: 96.3% of clicks.
  • Tablets: 1.4% of clicks.
  • Computers: 0.8% of clicks.
  • TV screens: 1.5% of clicks.

Impression & cost distribution

  • Impressions: 55.4% mobile, 11.3% tablet, 3.8% computer, 29.5% TV.
  • Cost: 42.4% mobile and 48.0% TV screens, with tablets at 5.1% and computers at 4.5%.
  • This shows a clear distinction between where the ads were seen and where clicks were generated.
Audience & timing

The supplied screenshots show broad age/gender delivery and stronger impression activity from late morning into evening.

Demographics

  • The demographic heatmap is based on 73% of impressions with known age and gender.
  • The screenshot shows relatively strong male impression intensity across 25–34 through 65+.
  • Female delivery is also visible across the age groups, with comparatively lighter intensity in the older segments.
  • These are impression patterns, not proof of audience preference or conversion quality.

Day & hour

  • The impression heatmap shows delivery throughout the week.
  • Activity is visibly stronger from roughly late morning through evening.
  • The darkest cells appear around afternoon/evening windows on several days.
  • The screenshot is impression-based, so it should not be treated as a conversion-based schedule recommendation.
