Combining high-intent Search with local Performance Max.

This case study documents a Google Ads program for an immigration consultancy serving Surrey and broader Canadian demand. Search campaigns captured high-intent immigration queries, while Performance Max supported local visibility and additional conversion actions around the Surrey office.

Search insight

High-intent local and service queries combined efficiency with volume.

  • “immigration near me” delivered 20 conversions at about CA$6.01 per conversion.
  • apply for express entry canada delivered 20 conversions at CA$7.39 with 3.52× conversion value / cost.
  • immigration consultants was the highest-volume combined keyword cluster at 36 conversions.
  • Brand searches also generated conversions, but lower reported value/cost means they should be analysed separately from non-brand acquisition terms.

Performance Max asset group

One local asset group generated 793 platform-tracked conversions.

Asset Group 1 · Surrey immigration services

  • 137,097 impressions and 2,837 interactions.
  • 793 conversions at CA$2.41 cost per conversion.
  • Headlines covered PR, work permits, study permits, family sponsorship, spousal sponsorship and RCIC consultation.
  • Search themes included Canada PR, Express Entry, immigration Surrey, work permit visa and immigration services near me.

Why the structure matters

  • Local PMax supported discovery across Google surfaces while Search handled explicit high-intent queries.
  • Service-specific headlines and search themes broadened query coverage without forcing every intent into one Search ad group.
  • The two channels should be evaluated with separate conversion-quality checks because their platform conversion actions can differ.
