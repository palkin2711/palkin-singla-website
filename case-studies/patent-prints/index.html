Scaling purchase volume without losing sight of efficiency.

This case study turns a large Meta Ads account export into a clear performance story—showing where purchase volume came from, which campaign and creative structures were more efficient, and where newer tests needed tighter control.

Account-level performance

A mature purchase account with meaningful scale.

The export contains 34 named campaigns, 127 ad sets and 487 named ads. Campaign-level purchases sum to 2,232 across $131,194.02 in spend.

The account challenge

Scale was concentrated—but efficiency varied widely by structure.

  • CBO - SCALING carried more than half of all documented purchases.
  • Testing and retargeting delivered lower cost per purchase than the scaling campaign.
  • DPA supplied volume, while selected static creatives delivered stronger unit economics at lower scale.
  • Recent 2026 tests show why new campaigns need fast creative and budget feedback loops.
How I would manage the account

Protect winners, isolate tests and read purchase quality at every level.

  • Separate evergreen scaling, testing, DPA, seasonal and retargeting structures.
  • Compare purchase cost and ROAS together—not purchase cost alone.
  • Promote winning creatives from test environments into controlled scaling.
  • Watch frequency in retargeting and refresh creative before efficiency decays.

Testing was more efficient than scaling.

CBO - TESTING recorded a $45.24 cost per purchase versus $51.12 for CBO - SCALING—about 11.5% lower—while its reported ROAS was 1.96× versus 1.74×.

Retargeting remained efficient, but frequency was much higher.

Retargeting recorded $45.13 cost per purchase and 2.23× ROAS. Its campaign frequency was 5.25, so creative fatigue and audience saturation would need closer monitoring.

Ad set & creative winners

DPA delivered volume; selected statics delivered stronger efficiency.

Ad set comparison

Statics (12/8) beat the largest DPA ad set on efficiency.

DPA - All Products - Copy 2 generated 509 purchases at $51.65 per purchase and 1.76× ROAS. Statics (12/8) - Open generated 137 purchases at $40.46 and 2.30× ROAS.

  • Cost per purchase was about 21.7% lower for the static ad set.
  • Reported ROAS was about 30.5% higher.
  • The DPA set still delivered much greater purchase volume, so the comparison is an efficiency benchmark—not proof that one format should fully replace the other.

Creative comparison

Static LEGO creative set a strong purchase-cost benchmark.

Static - LEGO Man + LEGO Brick recorded 112 purchases at $37.07 per purchase with 2.51× ROAS. The highest-volume DPA - All Products ad recorded 509 purchases at $51.65 with 1.76× ROAS.

  • The LEGO static's cost per purchase was about 28.2% lower.
  • Its reported ROAS was about 42.4% higher.
  • Video also scaled: Vid - Amina Broll w/ Music produced 298 purchases at $50.65 per purchase.
