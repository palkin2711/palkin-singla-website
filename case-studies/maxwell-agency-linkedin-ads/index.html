Maxwell Agency · LinkedIn Ads · B2B lead generation

Testing a Free Group Benefits Review with US business decision-makers.

This six-day LinkedIn Lead Generation campaign promoted a free review of existing group insurance benefits. The strategy used pain-point-led messaging, a native LinkedIn Lead Gen Form and two creative concepts aimed at business decision-makers in the United States.

Campaign performance

A short B2B test that generated one verified LinkedIn lead.

The campaign ran from September 21–26, 2026 with a $100/day ad-set budget and CPM bidding. During the reporting period it spent $591.24, generated 8,618 impressions and 32 clicks, and opened the LinkedIn Lead Gen Form 20 times.

Campaign structure

One decision-maker ad set, two creative directions and one native Lead Gen Form.

Audience & setup

  • Campaign: Maxwell | Group Insurance | Lead Gen | Sep 2026.
  • Ad set: Maxwell | Group Insurance | Decision Makers | US | Sep 2026.
  • Objective: Lead generation.
  • Ad format: Sponsored Update with LinkedIn Lead Gen Form.
  • Daily budget: $100.
  • Cost type: CPM.

Offer & positioning

  • Primary offer: Free Group Benefits Review.
  • Review existing group insurance plans for potential savings.
  • Identify possible coverage gaps.
  • Explore options aligned with the workforce.
  • Use a problem/solution message rather than generic insurance promotion.
Creative test

Creative 1 generated the only recorded lead during the reporting period.

The two ads were both active in the same ad set. Creative 1 received more delivery and produced the campaign's single recorded lead. Because this was only six days of data, the result is presented as an early signal—not a definitive long-term winner.

What the creative data supports

Pain-point messaging produced the only completed lead.

Creative 1 focused on the cost/coverage problem and generated the campaign's only recorded submission. Creative 2 used a more general employee-benefits review message and did not record a lead in this short window.

  • Creative 1 accounted for 77.8% of campaign spend and 78.1% of clicks.
  • Its form-open rate from clicks was 64% (16 of 25).
  • Creative 2 produced 4 form opens from 7 clicks, but no completed form.
  • The next optimization question would be whether the form itself, offer framing or audience qualification could improve the 5% overall completion rate.
