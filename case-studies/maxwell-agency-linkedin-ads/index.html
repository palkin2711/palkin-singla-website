11 LinkedIn leads across five group-benefits lead-generation campaigns.

This updated case study summarizes the five filtered LinkedIn lead-generation campaigns shown in the supplied account reports through September 30, 2026. The campaigns promoted group insurance and employee-benefits offers to US business decision-makers using Sponsored Content and LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms.

Overall performance

Five selected campaigns generated 11 recorded LinkedIn leads.

Across the five campaigns shown in the campaign-level report, LinkedIn recorded $11,242.03 in spend, 124,782 impressions, 755 clicks and 11 leads. The blended click-through rate was 0.61%, average CPC was $14.89 and blended cost per lead was $1,022.00.

What the data supports

Specific offers outperformed generic benefit messaging in the shown ads.

The lowest CPL in the supplied ad report came from the direct proposal offer, while broader workforce-benefit messaging required materially more spend per lead. This supports continued testing around concrete outcomes, direct offers and lower-friction Lead Gen Forms.

  • Best shown ad CPL: $250.55 on “Request your free group benefits proposal”.
  • Best shown ad-set CPL: $376.58 on the Aug 18 lead-generation ad set.
  • Best shown ad-set CTR: 0.76% on the Jul 20 ad set.
  • September campaign: 1 lead from $943.51 spend, with 0.37% CTR.

Campaign strategy

Lead generation was built around native forms and decision-maker messaging.

Core setup

  • Channel: LinkedIn Ads.
  • Objective: Lead generation.
  • Primary format: Sponsored Content with LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms.
  • Market: United States.
  • Offer themes: group benefits review, cost reduction, coverage improvement and proposal requests.

Optimization direction

  • Prioritize the strongest offer-led creative concepts.
  • Separate HR/benefits decision-makers from broad owner/founder targeting.
  • Keep lead forms short and reduce friction after the click.
  • Use ad-set and creative CPL, not CTR alone, to guide budget decisions.
  • Continue testing direct proposal/review offers against cost-saving angles.
