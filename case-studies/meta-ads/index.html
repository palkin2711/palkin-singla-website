How to read the work

Performance is shown with the context needed to interpret it.

Purchase campaigns, message campaigns, calls and appointments use different success metrics. The individual case studies therefore compare like-for-like results where possible and clearly label cross-campaign comparisons, attribution differences and unusual platform-reported values.

Reporting note: the pages use Meta Ads Manager exports and screenshots supplied for each client account. Platform reporting can vary by attribution setting and reporting level, so the case studies avoid presenting mixed objectives as one combined conversion total.
