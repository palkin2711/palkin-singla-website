eCommerce SEO

Growing category, product and informational visibility for a raised-garden-bed brand.

The Vego Garden project used product-focused keyword research, site architecture, product-page optimization, on-page SEO, technical SEO, speed improvement, backlinks and content marketing. The supplied report includes Search Console comparisons plus SE Ranking, SEMrush, Analytics and authority snapshots.

Search architecture

  • Raised garden beds and modular planter product terms.
  • Category and product-page optimization.
  • Informational content supporting product discovery.
  • Internal architecture and authority building.

Commerce alignment

  • Connect high-volume garden searches to product/collection pages.
  • Use blog content to capture early-stage research intent.
  • Improve technical crawlability and speed for a growing store.
  • Build link authority in a competitive home-and-garden niche.
