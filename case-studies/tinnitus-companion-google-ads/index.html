Google App Campaign

Launching Android acquisition from a zero-data baseline under health-ad restrictions.

The supplied project report documents a Google Universal App Campaign for Tinnitus Companion between May 1–26, 2026. The account began without prior conversion history, while health-category policy limitations constrained delivery.

Execution

  • Android App Install campaign with a £20/day budget.
  • Maximize Installs bidding and app-install conversion tracking.
  • Multiple headlines, descriptions, images and video assets.
  • Policy-aware messaging around tinnitus support without prohibited claims.
  • Daily CPI, pacing and install-volume monitoring.

Measured result

  • 342 installs in the documented 26-day period.
  • £607.55 spend and £1.78 CPI.
  • 62,467 impressions and 2,254 clicks.
  • £0.27 avg. CPC and 14.87% click-to-install rate.
Campaign performance

The core CPI target was achieved during the first month of live delivery.
