How results are presented

SEO evidence is kept source-specific instead of forcing every project into one metric.

Search Console, Analytics, rank trackers, third-party SEO tools and AI-citation platforms each measure different things. These case studies report the evidence as documented and do not turn unrelated date windows into artificial before/after percentages.

Reporting note: where historical GA4/GSC evidence is unavailable, the page clearly says so rather than inventing traffic or ranking gains. GEO evidence is also labelled separately from conventional Google rankings.
