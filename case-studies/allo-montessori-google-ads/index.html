Allo Montessori · Google Ads · Local education & family activities

Connecting local families with preschool, daycare, birthday parties and seasonal programs.

This case study examines the supplied Google Ads account exports and screenshots across Montessori and preschool discovery, birthday-party bookings, summer programs and Prep & Play classes. It separates documented account results from interpretations and does not attribute conversions to campaigns where campaign-level conversion data was not supplied.

Preschool & local search

The Montessori campaign concentrated spend on local education discovery.

Allo Montessori - near me

  • $21,789.43 recorded spend, 3,644 clicks and 4.31% CTR.
  • The supplied keyword rows include “allo charlestown,” “allo montessori preschool,” “daycare near me” and “charlestown nursery school.”
  • Brand, competitor and category queries represent different search intents; the available files do not show their individual lead quality.

Top visible keyword rows

  • “allo charlestown”: $1,215.58 spend, 167 clicks, 23.13% CTR.
  • “allo montessori preschool”: $1,181.29 spend, 235 clicks, 25.21% CTR.
  • “daycare near me”: $941.00 spend, 137 clicks, 3.24% CTR.
  • A second “allo charlestown” row appears in the export; it is kept separate rather than silently combined.
Birthday parties & seasonal demand

Birthday-party campaigns supplied high click volume; summer Search showed a higher CTR.

Birthday-party campaigns

  • Birthday Party - SC: 4,777 clicks, $5,660.26 spend and 4.79% CTR.
  • Birthday Party: 4,362 clicks, $2,759.58 spend and 0.71% CTR.
  • The most-shown Birthday Party - SC ad shows 55,947 impressions, 2,713 clicks and 4.85% CTR; the ad is marked “Not eligible” in the supplied snapshot.

Summer and classes

  • Search Campaign Summer Camps: 780 clicks, $3,637.68 spend and 9.74% CTR.
  • Summer Programs: 1,306 clicks, $1,168.05 spend and 0.60% CTR.
  • Prep & Play Classes: 929 clicks, $1,276.56 spend and 1.62% CTR.
  • CTR is a traffic-engagement metric, not proof of registrations, qualified leads or bookings.
Search-query evidence

The recorded queries span brand, category, local and competitor discovery.

Queries with visible impression volume

  • “indoor playgrounds near me”: 3,412 impressions and 242 clicks.
  • “daycare near me”: 3,250 impressions and 101 clicks.
  • “indoor playground near me”: 2,740 impressions and 206 clicks.
  • “montessori school”: 2,456 impressions and 47 clicks.

Word-level patterns

  • “near me” appears in queries totaling 20,226 impressions in the supplied word report.
  • “montessori” appears in queries totaling 6,930 impressions; “daycare” appears in queries totaling 5,761 impressions.
  • Word-level rows overlap because a query can contain multiple words; they must not be summed into unique account totals.
  • The provided search-query export displays 0.00 conversions in its visible rows. This does not negate the overview’s 553.5 reported conversions; the reports have different scope and attribution.
Device & network mix

Mobile phones accounted for most clicks, while Google Search accounted for most recorded cost.

Devices · screenshot shares

  • Mobile phones: 75.4% of clicks, 73.2% of cost and 66.7% of impressions.
  • Tablets: 11.7% of clicks, 2.8% of cost and 7.0% of impressions.
  • Computers: 12.9% of clicks, 24.0% of cost and 26.2% of impressions.

Networks · CSV

  • Google Search: 10,683 clicks, $33,168.57 spend and $3.10 CPC.
  • Display network: 5,147 clicks, $3,100.17 spend and $0.60 CPC.
  • Search partners: 33 clicks, $118.23 spend and $3.58 CPC.
  • Network rows reconcile to the seven-campaign export totals for clicks and cost.
Audience & timing

The screenshots show daytime exposure and a concentration in known female age groups.

Day & hour

  • The impression heatmap is strongest during late morning through afternoon, with visibly darker cells around midday and early afternoon.
  • The heatmap is based on impressions, not conversions; it does not establish the best time to schedule ads.

Demographics

  • The screenshot’s darkest cells are among women aged 25–34 and 35–44.
  • The demographic breakdown is based on 73% of impressions with known age and gender.
  • The remaining impressions are not represented in that heatmap, so the pattern should be treated as directional.
Auction & historical context

Auction insights and the change report provide context, not conversion attribution.

Auction insights

  • The supplied chart plots impression share against top-of-page rate for the account and other advertisers participating in the same auctions.
  • The chart alone does not establish competitors’ budgets, lead volume, profitability or market share.

Biggest changes

  • The change export compares Oct 1, 2021–Sep 26, 2026 against Oct 5, 2016–Sep 30, 2021.
  • “preschool Near me” shows $12,978.41 in the newer period; “Montessori Near me” shows $8,811.02.
  • A zero comparison value does not, by itself, prove incremental performance or a causal improvement.
