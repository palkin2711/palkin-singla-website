KaamSey · LinkedIn Ads · B2B Lead Generation

Generating B2B leads across warehouse operations and environmental compliance.

KaamSey used LinkedIn Lead Generation campaigns to reach professionals around two operational use cases: warehouse auditing and environmental compliance. The campaigns promoted a mobile auditing platform focused on process consistency, visibility, real-time data collection and compliance-ready reporting.

Different B2B pain points produced different strengths.

  • Warehouse Operations generated 5 of the 8 leads and the lower CPL at $366.71.
  • Environmental Compliance generated the stronger CTR at 0.699% and a lower CPC of $13.48.
  • Both campaigns used LinkedIn Lead Generation and Sponsored Update ad sets.
  • The results support maintaining separate use-case messaging rather than merging both operational problems into one generic campaign.

Warehouse messaging

  • Focused on warehouse inefficiencies, audit consistency and visibility for operations leaders.
  • Promoted mobile auditing as a way to improve accuracy and process execution.
  • The main creative generated 5 leads and 77 form opens.

Compliance messaging

  • Focused on inefficient environmental audits and real-time data collection.
  • Positioned automated reporting and compliance-ready insights as the core value.
  • The creative generated 3 leads from 68 form opens.
Strategic read

The strongest opportunity was not getting people to open the form—it was getting more of those opens to become submissions.

Across both campaigns, 148 of 214 clicks opened the Lead Gen Form, a 69.2% click-to-form-open rate. Only 5.41% of form opens became leads, making the form-completion stage the clearest optimization opportunity in the supplied data.

What worked

  • Use-case-specific messaging created clear relevance for warehouse and compliance professionals.
  • Environmental Compliance produced the stronger CTR and CPC.
  • Warehouse Operations produced more total leads and the lower campaign CPL.
  • LinkedIn native forms generated substantial form-opening activity.

What to test next

  • Reduce friction in the Lead Gen Form where possible.
  • Test stronger value-exchange messaging before the form opens.
  • Separate high-intent operations titles from broader professional audiences.
  • Test proof-led creative around audit efficiency, compliance reporting and process visibility.
