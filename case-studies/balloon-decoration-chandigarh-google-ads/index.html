Balloon Decoration Chandigarh · Google Ads · Lead generation

Driving local demand for balloon decoration through high-intent Search.

This Google Ads Search campaign targeted people actively looking for birthday decoration, balloon decoration and nearby party-decoration services. The supplied account data shows 13.3K impressions, 383 clicks and ₹3.39K in spend during the reporting period.

Local Search strategy

The campaign was built around service-specific and occasion-specific searches.

  • The ad headline directly referenced Balloon Decoration Chandigarh, balloon decoration near me and same-day balloon decoration.
  • Ad sitelinks covered Birthday Decoration, Contact Us, Baby Shower Decoration and Kids Party Decoration.
  • The keyword export contains 50 enabled keyword rows, while the search-term export contains 100 reported queries.
  • The account generated 383 clicks at an average CPC of about ₹8.85, giving the campaign a clear local-demand acquisition role.

  • “1 happy birthday” and related birthday-message searches consumed spend but may be less commercially specific than decoration-service queries.
Device & network behavior

Traffic was overwhelmingly mobile, while Search Partners supplied a large share of clicks.

Devices

  • Mobile phones: 97.5% of cost, 99.6% of impressions and 99.5% of clicks.
  • Computers: 2.5% of cost, 0.3% of impressions and 0.5% of clicks.
  • Tablets: 0% of cost, 0.1% of impressions and 0% of clicks in the supplied widget.
  • The mobile-heavy mix makes mobile landing-page speed, click-to-call and WhatsApp/contact UX especially important for lead generation.

Networks

  • Google Search: 23.0% of clicks and 67.3% of cost, with a displayed average CPC of ₹25.91.
  • Search partners: 77.0% of clicks and 32.7% of cost, with a displayed average CPC of ₹3.76.
  • The very large click share from Search Partners should be evaluated against lead quality before treating low CPC as a success signal.
  • Because the supplied report does not include network-level lead quality, this page does not claim which network generated better leads.
Audience timing & demographics

The campaign's visible activity concentrated in afternoon and evening hours.

Day & hour

  • The supplied heatmap shows the campaign serving primarily from the afternoon into evening.
  • The strongest visible blocks cluster around the mid-to-late afternoon period, with activity continuing into the evening.
  • Very little activity is visible in the overnight and morning sections of the supplied chart.
  • Any ad-schedule change should be validated against actual lead quality and call/contact data rather than impressions alone.

Demographics

  • The widget is based on the 84% of impressions with known gender and age.
  • The strongest visible concentration is among 25–34 and 35–44 users, particularly males in the heatmap.
  • Female 25–34 and 35–44 also show meaningful impression density.
  • The remaining 16% of impressions are outside the known demographic breakdown, so the chart should be treated as directional rather than complete.
Search ad relevance

The ad copy clearly positioned the service around Chandigarh and fast local decoration needs.

The most-shown ad uses service-led messaging such as “Balloon Decoration Chandigarh,” “balloon decoration near me” and “same day balloon decoration,” followed by supporting copy about birthdays, baby showers, events, home/hotel/surprise setups and quick service.
