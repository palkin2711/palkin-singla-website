Hope4Families · Google Ads · Course acquisition

Scaling high-intent search demand across anger management and court-focused education.

This case study documents a multi-service Google Ads account for Hope4Families, using Search and Performance Max to reach people researching anger management classes, domestic-violence courses, co-parenting programs and court-related educational requirements.

Search-term quality

The search-query data reveals both valuable service intent and clear negative-keyword opportunities.

The top searches included highly relevant terms such as “anger management classes,” “domestic violence classes near me,” “co parenting classes” and “court ordered anger management classes.” The same report also shows repeated “free” intent, which is useful for identifying traffic that may not match a paid-course offer.

High-intent themes

  • anger management classes — 115 clicks / 3,569 impressions
  • anger management classes near me — 30 clicks / 973 impressions
  • domestic violence classes near me — 57 clicks / 546 impressions
  • co parenting classes — 23 clicks / 345 impressions
  • court ordered anger management classes — 11 clicks / 265 impressions

Traffic-refinement opportunity

  • free anger management classes generated 41 clicks.
  • free anger management classes near me generated 36 clicks.
  • free online anger management classes with certificate generated 39 clicks.
  • These queries do not automatically mean poor traffic, but they provide a clear basis for offer-alignment and negative-keyword review.
Measurement note: the uploaded search-query export reports 0 conversions across the 100 displayed query rows. Therefore this case study uses those rows to analyse intent and traffic quality—not to attribute lead or enrollment outcomes.

Device & network behavior

Desktop dominated the account, while Search remained the primary network.

Devices

  • Computers: 80.7% of cost, 77.3% of impressions and 80.3% of clicks.
  • Mobile phones: 18.2% of cost, 21.6% of impressions and 18.7% of clicks.
  • Tablets: roughly 1% across the main device metrics.
  • The pattern indicates a predominantly desktop-led enrollment/research journey.

Networks

  • Google Search: 88.3% of clicks and 91.1% of cost, at $10.81 average CPC.
  • Cross-network: 11.7% of clicks and 8.9% of cost, at $7.96 average CPC.
  • This supports a Search-first acquisition strategy with Performance Max contributing incremental reach.
Timing & demographic pattern

Weekday daytime demand was strongest, with known impressions concentrated among women 25–54.

Day & hour

  • The heatmap shows the densest activity from late morning through afternoon on weekdays.
  • Monday through Thursday carry the most sustained intensity, especially around midday.
  • Overnight demand is visibly lighter.

Demographics

  • The demographic widget is based on 63% of impressions with known age and gender.
  • The strongest visible blocks sit among women 25–54.
  • Men 35–54 also represent a meaningful share of known impressions.
  • These segments are directional audience observations, not demographic targeting recommendations by themselves.
