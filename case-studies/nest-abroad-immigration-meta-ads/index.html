One immigration project, managed through two Meta ad accounts.

This case study combines the documented performance of NIS Canada NEW and Nest Abroad Immigration India. Instead of splitting the work into separate pages, both accounts are analysed together because they supported the same overall client project and the same core business goal: generating immigration and study-abroad inquiries through Meta.

Account overview

Two different account structures, one shared lead-generation objective.

The Canada-focused account leaned into high-volume message and custom-conversion testing, while the India account combined messaging, lead-form and call-driven campaigns. Because the project was the same, the correct way to present the work is a single consolidated case study with separate sections for each account.

What this combined case study shows

Same client project, different market structures, consistent inquiry-generation intent.

1. Message-first lead generation worked in both accounts.

  • NIS Canada NEW relied heavily on message and custom-conversion testing around masters, SOWP, PR and IELTS/CLB themes.
  • Nest Abroad Immigration India produced a mix of messaging conversations, call confirmations and lead-form conversions.

2. Offer naming mattered.

  • Campaigns tied to specific visa, PR, SOWP, masters and score-guarantee angles repeatedly appeared among the top performers.
  • This indicates the account structure was built around offer clarity rather than generic top-of-funnel copy alone.

3. Two accounts should not become two unrelated case studies.

  • Because both accounts belonged to the same business project, they are better presented as one consolidated engagement.
  • This page keeps the project story intact while still separating account-level proof and results.

4. The data supports practical scaling decisions.

  • High-volume low-cost clusters can be expanded first.
  • Underperforming themes can be cut or reworked based on the clear cost-per-result differences shown in the exports.
