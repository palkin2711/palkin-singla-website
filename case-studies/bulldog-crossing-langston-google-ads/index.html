Bulldog Crossing & Langston · Google Ads · Apartment & student housing

Driving high-volume apartment and student-housing search demand across Athens and Atlanta.

This case study analyzes the supplied Google Ads account data for Bulldog Crossing, Langston and related housing campaigns. It covers apartment leasing, UGA/off-campus housing, second-chance rentals and local apartment discovery, while separating documented account metrics from interpretation.

Campaign concentration

Langston Search Campaign and Bulldog Crossing account for the largest recorded spend.

Langston Search Campaign

  • $61,169.85 spend with 7,504.64 reported conversions.
  • Reported cost per conversion: $8.15.
  • The biggest-changes export identifies General Leasing and 2nd Chance OneApp as major historical ad groups within this campaign.
  • Visible search intent includes apartment, affordability and Atlanta/Athens housing queries.

Bulldog Crossing

  • $52,341.15 spend with 1,621.00 reported conversions.
  • Reported cost per conversion: $32.29.
  • The most-shown ad is associated with Off-Campus Housing Near Me.
  • The supplied ad screenshot shows 160,012 impressions, 11,307 clicks and 7.07% CTR for that ad snapshot.

Devices & audience

Mobile dominated the click mix, while the demographic screenshot shows stronger female representation in several working-age groups.

Devices · screenshot shares

  • Mobile phones: 89.1% of clicks, 90.2% of cost and 89.1% of conversions.
  • Computers: 9.1% of clicks, 9.1% of cost and 8.1% of conversions.
  • Tablets: 1.8% of clicks, 0.7% of cost and 0.9% of conversions.

Demographics · screenshot

  • The heatmap is based on 75% of impressions with known age and gender.
  • The darkest visible cells are among women aged 25–34 and 35–44.
  • Male 25–34 also shows a comparatively strong impression cell.
  • These are impression patterns, not proof that a demographic segment produces better leases or lead quality.
Timing & search competition

The account shows broad daytime activity and a measurable competitive auction environment.

Day & hour

  • The impression heatmap is active across all days and shows stronger intensity from morning through afternoon.
  • The darkest cells cluster around roughly 10 AM–4 PM.
  • The chart is based on impressions, so it should not be treated as a conversion-based scheduling recommendation.

Auction insights

  • The supplied auction chart places the account below the 15% impression-share line shown in the screenshot.
  • Other visible auction participants include rent.com and apartment-listing competitors.
  • Auction position alone does not establish relative lead quality, profitability or leasing outcomes.
Historical change

The biggest-changes report highlights where spend and traffic accumulated in the newer period.

Largest visible changes

  • Bulldog Crossing · Off-Campus Housing Near Me: $36,348.85 and 11,316 clicks.
  • Langston Search Campaign · General Leasing: $32,082.31 and 23,205 clicks.
  • Langston Search Campaign · 2nd Chance OneApp: $15,137.97 and 13,888 clicks.
  • Langston Apt. Near Me - SC: $14,515.35 and 10,854 clicks.

Comparison period

  • The report compares Jul 1, 2020–Sep 26, 2026 with Apr 5, 2014–Jun 30, 2020.
  • Several ad groups show $0 in the comparison period, so the displayed increases largely represent activity that was absent from the comparison rows.
  • The comparison report is descriptive; it does not prove incremental lift or causality.
Optimization & account structure

The supplied optimization scores show different levels of Google Ads recommendation coverage.

Optimization score

  • Langston Search Campaign: 95.7%
  • Bulldog Crossing: 83%
  • These scores are Google Ads account-interface metrics; they are not performance scores and should not be interpreted as profitability or lead-quality ratings.

Measurement caveat

  • The campaign export reports fractional conversions, indicating that Google Ads is using conversion actions that can produce non-integer totals.
  • The supplied files do not identify the conversion actions, their values or downstream leasing outcomes.
  • Accordingly, this case study documents advertising performance rather than verified lease or revenue attribution.
