How to read the work

Four signals I use to judge campaign performance.

No single metric tells the whole story. I look at traffic quality, conversion quality, efficiency and the next action together.

01

Traffic quality

Are the searches, audiences and visitors relevant to the offer and the customers the business actually wants?

02

Conversion quality

Are leads, purchases and enquiries moving toward qualified opportunities—not just increasing raw volume?

03

Efficiency

Are CPA, CPL, conversion rate, ROAS or other goal-specific metrics moving in a commercially useful direction?

04

Next action

What should be tested, scaled, reduced or fixed based on the evidence—not on assumptions?
