Dussehra Marketing Campaign Ideas 2026: Offers, Creatives and Remarketing

19 Oct 2026 · By Palkin Singla

Plan a stronger Dussehra 2026 campaign with relevant offers, creative themes, Google and Meta Ads, remarketing and conversion tracking.

Dussehra falls on October 20, 2026 in India. For many brands, it sits inside a broader festive buying period rather than acting as a one-day event. That makes it useful as a campaign milestone: a chance to refresh creative, introduce urgency and move warm audiences toward conversion before the larger Diwali shopping period.

Use Dussehra as a campaign moment, not just a greeting

A festive greeting can support brand presence, but paid media needs a clear business objective. Decide whether the campaign should generate sales, bookings, enquiries, store visits or remarketing engagement.

Campaign angles by industry

  • eCommerce: festive bundles, limited collections, free shipping thresholds.
  • Jewellery and fashion: celebration-ready products and gifting.
  • Beauty and wellness: pre-festival appointments and packages.
  • Home services: cleaning, décor, renovation or seasonal preparation.
  • Education: new-beginning positioning, but keep claims realistic.
  • B2B: year-end planning, seasonal business preparation or simple brand content.

Build a three-stage media plan

Stage 1: Warm-up

Introduce the offer and build video viewers, site visitors and product engagement before the final high-intent window.

Stage 2: Conversion push

Use direct CTAs, deadline language and high-intent search terms. Make delivery dates or booking availability clear.

Stage 3: Remarketing bridge to Diwali

Do not shut everything off immediately after Dussehra. Build remarketing audiences and continue the strongest products or offers into the next festive phase.

Creative ideas

  • “Festive refresh” before/after concepts
  • Customer favourites for the celebration period
  • Gift guides by budget
  • Limited-slot appointment creatives
  • Founder or team festive message paired with a practical offer
  • Countdown creatives where the deadline is genuine

Track incrementality, not only coupon use

Festival campaigns often overlap with existing demand. Compare results against normal periods, new-customer share and qualified lead or revenue outcomes. A high-discount campaign can show strong revenue while reducing margin.

Build the campaign around a real deadline

Urgency works best when it reflects something operational: last shipping date, final appointment slot, limited stock or campaign end date. Artificial countdowns can weaken trust. Make the deadline visible in the ad and repeat it on the landing page.

Use customer lists and first-party audiences carefully

Existing customers can be segmented for relevant cross-sell or repeat-purchase offers, while recent purchasers may need to be excluded from acquisition campaigns. Respect consent requirements and platform policies when using customer data.

After Dussehra: prepare the Diwali handoff

Review which products, locations, creatives and audiences performed best. Move the strongest learnings into the next campaign phase rather than rebuilding everything. Retarget visitors who engaged with relevant products and create fresh Diwali-specific messaging before creative fatigue appears.

FAQ

When should Dussehra ads start?

For products requiring delivery, start early enough to fulfil orders. Appointment businesses should launch before calendars fill. High-intent remarketing can become more urgent close to October 20.

Should I create a separate Dussehra landing page?

Only if the offer and content are meaningfully different. Otherwise, update a relevant collection or service page with a clear seasonal section.

Can Dussehra campaigns continue after the date?

The exact Dussehra messaging should end, but strong audiences and winning products can transition into Diwali-focused campaigns.
