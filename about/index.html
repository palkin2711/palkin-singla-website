How I work

A clear four-step process from audit to optimization.

The channel comes second. The first job is to understand the commercial objective, the customer journey and where performance is being lost.

01

Understand the goal

Define the business objective, target audience, offer, market and the outcome that should count as success.

02

Diagnose the gaps

Review campaigns, tracking, creative, landing pages, SEO visibility and follow-up to find the biggest constraints.

03

Execute the priorities

Implement the changes most likely to improve traffic quality, conversion efficiency and the customer journey.

04

Measure and improve

Use campaign, analytics and lead-quality data to explain what changed, what worked and what should happen next.
