Google Ads is giving advertisers more visibility into how AI Max for Search campaigns connects search terms, creative assets and landing pages. For lead-generation accounts, that matters because automation is only useful when you can still understand why performance changed.

Google announced on September 23, 2026 that a new AI Max reporting view is rolling out to provide a more unified look at the Search ads journey: which search terms triggered an ad, which assets the user saw and which landing page they reached. Google also said AI Brief is expanding to more languages, while additional availability details for the reporting feature will be shared later in the year.

What changed in AI Max reporting?

The practical change is better diagnostic visibility. Instead of reviewing search terms, assets and landing pages as separate pieces, advertisers should increasingly be able to evaluate the path as one connected journey.

  • Search term: what the user actually searched.
  • Creative combination: which headlines or assets were shown.
  • Landing destination: which page the user reached.
  • Outcome: whether the visit became a lead, qualified lead or sale.

Why this matters for lead-generation campaigns

Lead quality is usually lost somewhere between intent, message and landing-page fit. A broad query may reach the wrong page. A strong query may see a generic message. A relevant ad may lead to a page that does not explain eligibility, geography or the next step.

The new reporting direction makes it easier to ask better questions. For example: are weak leads coming from poor search intent, from the wrong creative promise, or from a landing page that is too broad?

Use the data to audit the full path

  1. Identify search terms generating the highest volume.
  2. Separate qualified from unqualified outcomes in your CRM or lead sheet.
  3. Compare which assets are associated with stronger-quality searches.
  4. Review the landing page used for each intent cluster.
  5. Add negatives, refine messaging or change destination pages where patterns are clear.

AI Max should not replace conversion-quality feedback

More reporting does not solve poor measurement. If Google Ads only receives a front-end form submission, it still cannot know whether that person became a qualified opportunity. For service businesses, connect campaign decisions to deeper outcomes such as qualified lead, appointment booked, proposal sent or customer.

My broader framework for this is covered in How to Improve Google Ads Lead Quality, where search intent, negative keywords, landing-page qualification and CRM feedback are treated as one system.

How to evaluate AI Max before scaling

Do not judge AI Max only by clicks or total conversions. Create a test framework that looks at:

  • Cost per qualified lead
  • Qualified lead rate
  • Search-term relevance
  • Conversion rate by landing page
  • Lead-to-opportunity rate
  • Revenue or pipeline value where available

Keep a control period

If you are changing targeting, creative and landing pages at the same time, you will struggle to understand what caused the result. Where possible, document a baseline before enabling major automation changes, then compare the same quality metrics after enough data has accumulated.

Where AI Brief fits

Google also said the closed beta of AI Brief is expanding to more languages. The idea is to let advertisers give AI Max more context about the business, audience and messaging. That can become useful when the product supports your language and account, but human inputs still need to be accurate. A vague business description can produce vague automation.

Practical action plan for advertisers

  • Confirm primary conversion actions are the outcomes you actually want Google to optimize.
  • Review search terms weekly and maintain negative keyword themes.
  • Keep landing pages tightly aligned with search intent.
  • Use CRM stages to distinguish a lead from a qualified lead.
  • Document changes before expanding AI Max.
  • Use new reporting to diagnose the full user journey, not as a replacement for business judgment.

FAQ

Is AI Max the same as broad match?

No. AI Max is a broader Search campaign capability that can use Google AI across targeting and creative features. Broad match is one keyword matching option.

Should every lead-generation advertiser enable AI Max?

Not automatically. Accounts with reliable conversion tracking, clear landing pages and strong lead-quality feedback are in a better position to test automation responsibly.

What metric matters most when testing AI Max?

For many service businesses, cost per qualified lead is more useful than raw cost per lead because it reflects whether the enquiries are actually relevant.

Will the new reporting show why every lead was poor?

No. Reporting can show the ad journey, but qualification still depends on CRM notes, sales follow-up and business-specific criteria.
