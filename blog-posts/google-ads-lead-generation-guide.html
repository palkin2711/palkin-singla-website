Google Ads can generate strong results for service businesses, but getting more leads is not enough. The focus should be on generating qualified leads that are more likely to convert into customers.

Focus on High-Intent Keywords

Choose keywords that indicate the user is actively searching for your service rather than simply researching the topic.

Review Search Terms Regularly

Search term analysis helps identify irrelevant traffic and new opportunities.

  • Add irrelevant searches as negative keywords.
  • Identify high-converting search terms.
  • Separate strong keywords into focused ad groups.
  • Monitor qualified lead quality, not only form submissions.

Improve Your Landing Page

Keep the landing page closely aligned with the keyword and ad message. Use a clear offer, strong CTA and simple enquiry process.

Track Qualified Conversions

Campaign optimization should be based on meaningful leads and sales rather than clicks alone.
