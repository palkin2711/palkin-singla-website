My process

Audit first. Prioritize next. Scale what proves itself.

The goal is to make the next decision clearer—not to add more marketing activity without a reason.

01

Understand the goal

Clarify the commercial target, audience, market, offer and current performance.

02

Audit the journey

Review targeting, search intent, creative, tracking, landing pages, SEO and follow-up.

03

Execute the priority work

Fix the highest-impact constraint first and build a controlled testing plan.

04

Measure & refine

Use qualified outcomes, search data and conversion evidence to decide what changes next.

Quick answers about my role and digital marketing work.

These answers clarify who Palkin Singla is, what a digital marketing specialist does and how I work with businesses.

Who is Palkin Singla?

Palkin Singla is a digital marketing specialist based in India with 7+ years of hands-on experience across SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, content, analytics and conversion-focused web execution. Palkin is the short name used across this personal portfolio.

What does a digital marketing specialist do?

A digital marketing specialist plans, executes and measures online growth across channels such as SEO, paid advertising, content, social media, email, analytics and websites. The exact channel mix depends on the business goal.

Which digital marketing services does Palkin provide?

I provide SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn marketing, TikTok marketing, social media, email marketing, content marketing, website design and website development.

Can Palkin manage both SEO and paid advertising?

Yes. I can manage a focused channel or connect SEO and paid acquisition with landing pages, conversion tracking, content and reporting when the project needs an integrated approach.

Where does Palkin work with clients?

I am based in Ambala, Haryana, India and work remotely with businesses, founders and agencies in India and international markets including the UK and Canada.
