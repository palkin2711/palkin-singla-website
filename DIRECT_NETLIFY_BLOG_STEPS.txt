DIRECT NETLIFY BLOG PUBLISHING (NO GITHUB)

ONE-TIME:
1. Keep this complete website folder on your computer. This is your MASTER folder.
2. Install Node.js 20+ if it is not already installed.

FOR EVERY NEW BLOG:
1. Open the blog-posts folder.
2. Copy _template.html and rename it, e.g. google-ads-lead-quality.html.
3. Edit title, description, date, category, feature image URL, and article body.
4. Save the HTML file.
5. Double-click BUILD_BLOG.bat.
6. Wait for SUCCESS.
7. Open Netlify > your site > Deploys.
8. Choose manual deploy / drag-and-drop deploy.
9. Upload or drag the COMPLETE website folder (not only the new HTML file).
10. After Published, check /blog/ and open the new article.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not edit files inside /blog/ manually. They are generated automatically.
- Do not delete /assets/, /scripts/, package.json, or netlify.toml.
- Keep the previous working Netlify deploy available so you can restore if needed.
