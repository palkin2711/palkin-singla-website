Before you send

Four details that make the first review more useful.

You do not need a long brief. These four points are usually enough to understand the starting point and recommend the right next step.

01

Your goal

For example: qualified leads, sales, stronger organic visibility, better tracking or a website improvement.

02

Your market

Share the countries, cities or customer segments you want to reach.

03

Current setup

Include the website and the platforms you are already using, if applicable.

04

Main challenge

Explain what is not working now, such as poor lead quality, low ROAS, weak rankings or conversion issues.
