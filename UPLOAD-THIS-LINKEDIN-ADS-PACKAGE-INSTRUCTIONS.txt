PALKIN SINGLA — LINKEDIN ADS CASE STUDIES FINAL UPLOAD
======================================================

This package adds:
1. LinkedIn Ads Case Studies hub
2. Maxwell Agency LinkedIn Ads case study
3. KaamSey LinkedIn Ads case study
4. LinkedIn Ads Case Studies item in the global Case Studies dropdown
5. LinkedIn Ads collection block on /case-studies/

UPLOAD METHOD
-------------
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the CONTENTS of the extracted folder into the ROOT of your existing website repository.
3. Allow Windows/Git to MERGE matching folders.
4. Replace assets/js/site.js when asked. This is intentional.
5. Commit and push to main. Netlify should redeploy automatically.

DO NOT upload the outer ZIP folder itself as a nested website folder.
